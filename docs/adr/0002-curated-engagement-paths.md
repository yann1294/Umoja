# ADR 0002 - Curated individual and team engagement paths

- Status: Accepted for public positioning; contract models remain pending legal approval
- Date: 2026-09-02
- Scope: Public positioning, intake routing, profile discovery, and responsibility claims

## Decision

Umoja presents two curated client paths:

1. Request an approved African technology professional for an individual engagement.
2. Request Umoja's help assembling a complete expert team.

Both paths require human qualification. Neither creates an open bidding marketplace, unrestricted
freelancer directory, public rates or contact details, opaque ranking, automated contracting,
escrow, or payouts. Every public profile remains opt-in, moderated, and limited to approved public
fields.

Umoja may introduce a professional, help assemble a client-directed team, or contract for managed
delivery. Those models have different responsibility boundaries. Public copy must not claim that
Umoja or the client/employer always bears delivery or legal responsibility. The approved contract
must identify the parties, delivery authority, subcontracting disclosure, liability, tax,
employment classification, intellectual property, jurisdiction, and acceptance model.

## Consequences

- The homepage leads with hiring and gives individual and team choices before institutional detail.
- Hiring actions begin a qualified Umoja intake; they never reveal private professional data.
- Detailed Core, Extended, partner, manifesto, and AfricIT narratives live under About.
- Legal, tax, subcontracting, employment, and jurisdiction decisions remain Gate C owner decisions.
- The Phase 2 workspace must model the approved engagement and contract version explicitly rather
  than infer responsibility from whether one professional or a team is assigned.
